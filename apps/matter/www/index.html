Read devices on RS-485

With RS-485 set to master, each row is one value this device reads by itself and, with a Matter type, reports on a Matter sensor endpoint. Scale the value to the unit of its type: °C, % (humidity), hPa, m³/h, W, kWh (a meter reading). Reading only; nothing is ever written to the other device. A changed register or scale applies on save; a changed Matter type, or a row switched on or off, after a reboot.

The register number is the zero-based one from the device's manual, not the 4xxxx form. If a 32-bit value comes out as nonsense, tick Swap.
